# Isaac Teng's Personal Site

I am the founder of [IREducation Inc.](https://ireducation.org), a Hong Kong-based ed-tech firm, and also a MSci Mathematics and Physics student at [University College London (UCL)](https://ucl.ac.uk). I am the lead programmer for our products, with my main expertise lying in PHP development and backend server management, including deployment and maintenance of web applications.

Alongside this we have developed a suite of AI applications with core integration within our products - for this we have been awarded a grant by the Google Startup Program. Ultimately, our goal is to harness AI not just to bridge existing educational gaps, but to actively shape the future of learning through innovation, accessibility, and academic excellence.

I have also been the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of [Kovi Inc.](https://kovi.com.hk), a Hong Kong and Shenzhen based fashion start-up between October 2024 and December 2025.

In addition to my entrepreneurial experiences, I am a columnist for the [Hong Kong Economic Journal (HKEJ)](https://monthly.hkej.com) on emerging technological trends and hardware development. I have also taken up external consultancy work for private schools and university applicants.

For more information or corrections on my website, please contact me at [isaacteng@ireducation.org](mailto:isaacteng@ireducation.org). I look forward to hearing from you.
