Isaac Teng (鄧哲恆)

Founder · Author · Educational Technology Developer

I am the founder and lead developer of IREducation Ltd., a Hong Kong-based educational publishing and technology company whose UK activities are developed through Oxford Advanced Learning. I lead product strategy, software architecture, and curriculum development across IRMaths and IREducation’s wider print and digital portfolio. I am also an MSci Mathematics and Physics student at University College London (UCL).

My technical work spans full-stack web development, backend architecture, Linux server deployment, data and AI pipelines, and adaptive-learning systems. IREducation has received support through the Google for Startups programme, and our work combines interactive lessons, adaptive assessment, AI-supported feedback, and performance analytics.

I am also the author of Advanced Statistics for Edexcel A-Level: Core, Further & STEP in One Volume, a 22-chapter textbook used by students at more than ten schools, including several that have adopted it as a main teaching resource. I am currently preparing a seven-title Further Mathematics for CCEA A-Level series covering the complete Pure Mathematics course and every optional pathway; the Statistics volume is scheduled for late 2026, with the wider series expected in 2027.

Beyond education, I passed CFA Program Level I in 2026 and completed a summer internship in Valuation, Modelling & Economics at EY Hong Kong. I also write a column for the Hong Kong Economic Journal on emerging technology and societal trends, and undertake consultancy work for private schools and university applicants.

For more information or corrections on my website, please contact me at isaacteng@ireducation.org. I look forward to hearing from you.

IRMaths by IREducation

IRMaths is a live Edexcel A-Level Mathematics platform combining structured lessons across Pure Mathematics, Statistics, and Mechanics with adaptive assessment, step-by-step solutions, and performance analytics.

Its context-aware tutor can explain the lesson or question being viewed, check working, provide assessment-safe hints, and generate targeted follow-up practice. Completed assessment evidence supports focused remediation for students and class-level insights for teachers.
