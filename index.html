TeacherSearch

Our Teacher Applicational Platform (TAP) addresses inefficiencies in educator recruitment. Designed with teachers in mind, this platform eliminates the need for repetitive, manual form-filling across different schools and institutions. Instead, educators can create a comprehensive, centralised profile detailing their qualifications, experience, and teaching preferences. Schools will then be able to search, filter, and connect directly with suitable candidates who have expressed interest in their vacancies. This approach not only saves time for teachers but also improves efficiency for school hiring processes — ultimately fostering better matches between schools and educators. The platform will be particularly beneficial for state schools facing staffing shortages, providing them with access to a broad and diverse pool of qualified candidates.

IR-AI: Try IRMaths Helper

This is a highly advanced, interactive and personalised AI tutor, designed to retain user memory and adapt dynamically to each student’s learning style, pace, and academic goals. Scheduled for launch before March 2026, this AI system will be integrated across all of our educational platforms, including IRMaths, IRChemistry, and IRPhysics. It will also be offered as a standalone model for institutional licensing and commercial sale.

Built on advanced natural language processing and reinforcement learning, the system provides real-time feedback, adaptive difficulty progression, and exam-focused coaching. Designed with ethical AI principles at its core, it also includes features to support SEND learners, ensuring accessibility and inclusivity for a diverse student population.
