Featured publication

The first dedicated textbook for Edexcel A-Level Statistics

Written for the standalone Edexcel A-Level Statistics qualification (9ST0), which had no textbook of its own. It covers the full course, from foundational probability to advanced inference, with supplementary material for A-Level Mathematics, Further Mathematics, and STEP.

The book combines worked examples, misconception warnings, graded exercises, mixed reviews, and three complete sample papers with detailed solutions. “Beyond the Syllabus” sections connect the course to Bayesian statistics, machine learning, quantum mechanics, and financial mathematics.

Developed in conjunction with IRMaths, it can be used alongside a complete set of lesson slides, assignments, and tests. Published by IREducation Inc.

IRMaths by IREducation

IRMaths is our online platform for Edexcel A-Level Mathematics, with lessons across Pure Mathematics, Statistics, and Mechanics, practice questions, and worked solutions.

Its built-in tutor can explain whatever lesson or question a student is looking at, check their working, and give hints without handing over answers during assessments. Teachers can see where their class is struggling.
